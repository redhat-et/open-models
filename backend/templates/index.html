Notes & help

🧭 Which model should I pick?

core — Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next: your default. 4x L40S cluster, ~33 tok/s, good at everything daily, always up.

frontier — GLM-4.5-Air: hardest agentic / long-horizon work. It "thinks" first, so answers take longer; the box is powered down when idle, so first call after a quiet stretch can be slow or need the ⏻ Wake button.

bulk — Qwen 3.8 27B: background jobs, batch runs, high volume — cheap notional rates, runs on the single 5090.

lab — DGX Sparks: experiments. Direct endpoints over VPN only (see URL on the card), no SLA, no metering.

🪑 What are "seats"?

Live generation slots on that machine — the counter shows how many are busy right now. Near full? Requests still work, they just queue a bit. Consistently full everywhere is exactly what we watch for: say so in #octo-open-model-platform and we add capacity.

🧯 Something's slow, red, or erroring?

Red dot on a card = that endpoint is down; the gateway usually fails traffic over to a sibling automatically. Lab cards need the VPN. Hit a rate limit (429)? Limits are generous defaults — ping #octo-open-model-platform and we raise yours. Everything green but broken? Tell us in #octo-open-model-platform.

🔑 Keys, costs & privacy

Keys are personal — create one above, one per machine if you like. Nothing is billed to you; token counts exist only to compute "saved vs frontier APIs" and to size capacity. Nothing is stored per-request beyond LiteLLM's spend log.
